# Match the creative work to its domain

Use the relevant slice; do not load every discipline into the output.

**Content, video, or writing:** Start with the audience's reason to care and the intended payoff. Make the opening, structure, and examples concrete. Respect format, length, voice, and production constraints. Improve the idea before multiplying variations. Claims about retention or conversion are hypotheses until measured.

**Brand or campaign:** Give a clear positioning angle, distinctive message, and an example of how it appears in the chosen channel. Explain which audience it serves. Check coherence between the promise and the experience; do not invent an entire rebrand when the user needs a single campaign.

**Product, service, or side project:** Describe who has the problem, what they do today, and the smallest version that would improve it. Identify the assumption that could invalidate the concept. Prefer a small demonstrator, manual trial, or conversation with appropriate users before heavy construction when that fits the goal.

**Personal, learning, or community projects:** Work within the actual time and energy budget. Define a first finished unit and a practical signal of usefulness, enjoyment, participation, or learning. Monetization and scale are optional goals, not default requirements.
