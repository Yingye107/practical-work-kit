# Code of Conduct

Help make issues, discussions, and pull requests respectful and useful for people with different experience, language, background, and abilities.

- Critique the work and explain your reasoning. Avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, and unwanted sexual attention.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal information, private messages, or credentials.
- Keep participation relevant and constructive. No spam, impersonation, or pressure to grant access or buy services.
- Accept reasonable feedback and maintainer decisions. Disagreement about an idea is welcome when expressed respectfully.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close a disruptive discussion, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportional to the behavior and its effect on others.

For a conduct concern, use a private contact method currently available on the [maintainer's profile](https://github.com/Yingye107). If none is available, use GitHub's reporting tools; do not reveal private details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to participation in this repository and its project spaces.
